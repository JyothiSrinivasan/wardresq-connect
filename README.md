**WardResQ Connect** is a prototype flood emergency coordination platform designed to improve communication between citizens, municipal authorities and disaster response teams during urban flooding.

It creates a shared incident management environment where flood emergencies can be reported, prioritised, assigned and tracked.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** This is a hackathon demonstration prototype. It is not an operational emergency service and must not be used for real emergency response.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

During urban floods, emergency information is often fragmented across phone calls, messaging groups and disconnected reporting systems.

This can result in:
- Delayed identification of critical emergencies.
- Poor coordination between response teams.
- Difficulty identifying vulnerable people.
- Delayed allocation of rescue resources.
- Limited visibility into incident progress.

## 💡 Our Solution

WardResQ Connect provides a unified digital coordination platform that brings citizen incident reporting and emergency response management together.

The system uses an explainable prioritisation algorithm to help identify incidents requiring urgent attention.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Emergency Dashboard:** Overview of reported incidents, critical cases and people requiring assistance.
- **Incident Command & Dispatch:** Centralised incident table with search, filters and priority sorting.
- **Resident Incident Reporting:** Submit flood emergency reports with location, water depth, trapped-person and medical information.
- **Citizen WhatsApp Simulation:** Demonstrates a conversational reporting experience.
- **AI-Assisted Rescue Prioritisation:** Explainable scoring based on flood severity, trapped people, vulnerable demographics and medical emergencies.
- **Responder Assignment:** Assign response teams to incidents.
- **Rescue Lifecycle Tracking:** Track incidents through New, Assigned, En Route, Rescued and Resolved stages.
- **Priority Audit Breakdown:** Display the factors contributing to each incident's score and recommended resources.

## 🧠 Rescue Prioritisation

The prototype uses a deterministic, explainable scoring algorithm with a maximum score of 100.

Its assessment considers:

1. Water inundation severity.
2. Number of people trapped.
3. Vulnerable demographics, including children, elderly people and persons with disabilities.
4. Medical emergencies.

The system generates a priority category and recommends a suitable response resource.
